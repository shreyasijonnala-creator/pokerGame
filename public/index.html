How Buy-In Board works

This app tracks $20 buy-ins only. It doesn't handle cash-outs or payments.

If you're hosting

  1. Tap Create a table, name your game, and tap Open the table. You get a 5-character code.
  2. Tap the pencil next to the game name to rename it any time. Read out or copy the code for your players.
  3. Players join from their own phones. They appear in the seats as they join (15 seats max).
  4. Someone without a phone? Type their name under Add a player and tap Add player to give them a seat.
  5. Added someone by mistake? Tap × on their seat to remove them and any buy-ins they have.
  6. Tap + Add Buy-In each time a player buys in. Each tap adds $20.
  7. Tapped by mistake? Tap − to take one buy-in back.
  8. Playing too? Tap ← Home, then Join a table with your own code. The game keeps running, and Return to your table on the home screen takes you back.
  9. When the night is over, tap End game. Everyone sees the final totals.

If you're playing

  1. Tap Join a table and type the host's code.
  2. Enter your name and tap Take a seat.
  3. Watch your buy-ins and the pot update live. Only the host can add buy-ins.
  4. Heading out early? Tap Leave game. Your buy-ins stay on the host's books.

Past games

  • When the host taps End game, the game is saved to Past games on the home screen, for the host and for every player.
  • Each one shows the date, the final pot and everyone's buy-ins.
  • Past games are tied to this browser on this device.

The math

  • Player total = buy-ins × $20
  • Game total = every player's total added together
